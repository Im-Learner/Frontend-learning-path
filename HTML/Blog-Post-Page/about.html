About Me

Hello, I'm Reza Ashrafi. I have a background in Computer Engineering and a strong interest in software development, artificial intelligence and emerging technologies.

I am interested in understanding how technology works, how different fields connect and how new ideas can be transformed into useful products and solutions.

This website is a place to document ideas, explore technical topics, share research, and examine the opportunities and challenges created by technological progress.

My Approach

I believe technology should be understood beyond its surface-level applications. Learning how systems work, examining their limitations and thinking critically about their consequences are just as important as learning how to use them.

I aim to approach technology with curiosity, critical thinking, continuous learning and a focus on practical applications.

Why This Blog?

The technology landscape changes quickly. New tools, programming approaches and intelligent systems create both opportunities and difficult questions.

Through this blog, I explore these developments, share perspectives and try to understand what they could mean for developers, businesses and society.
